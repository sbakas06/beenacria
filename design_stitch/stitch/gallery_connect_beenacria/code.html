Where is your honey produced?

add

Our honey is exclusively harvested in the untouched, biodiverse regions of Sicily. We partner directly with master beekeepers who practice sustainable apiculture, ensuring every drop reflects the pristine Mediterranean terroir.

How should I store artisanal honey?

add

Keep it at room temperature in a dry, dark place. Never refrigerate honey, as cold temperatures accelerate crystallization. If crystallization occurs, simply place the jar in warm water to return it to a liquid state.

Is your honey raw and organic?

add

Yes. We never heat, pasteurize, or micro-filter our honey. It arrives in your jar exactly as the bees created it, preserving all natural pollens, enzymes, and nuanced flavor profiles indicative of its biological origin.

Do you offer international shipping?

add

We currently ship across Europe and North America using specialized temperature-controlled packaging to ensure the product's integrity upon arrival. Rates and times vary by destination.
