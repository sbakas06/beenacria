# Project Brief: Beenacria - Artisanal Sicilian Honey

## Brand Vision
Beenacria is a premium B2C artisanal honey producer rooted in Sicilian tradition but presented through a modern, luxury lens. The brand identity, "Liquid Gold Editorial," balances the warmth of artisanal craftsmanship with the sophistication of high-end editorial design.

## Core Objective
To create an immersive, high-fidelity digital presence that tells the story of Beenacria's honey—from the volcanic soils and floral diversity of Sicily to the traditional techniques preserved across generations.

## Target Audience
- Premium food enthusiasts and "gourmet" consumers.
- Design-conscious shoppers looking for authentic, luxury gifts.
- B2B partners (high-end boutiques, restaurants) interested in heritage products.

## Design System: Liquid Gold Editorial
- **Visual Direction**: Editorial, luxury boutique, high contrast.
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Primary: Vibrant Honey Gold/Amber (#ffb400).
  - Backgrounds: Rich Charcoal and Deep Honey-comb Brown (#131412).
  - Accents: Cream and Translucent Glassmorphism.
- **Typography**: 
  - Headlines: Playfair Display (Sophisticated Serif).
  - Body/Labels: Technical Sans-Serif (Modern contrast).
- **Key Motifs**: Organic fluid masks (mimicking dripping honey), honeycomb geometry, and high-resolution cinematic photography.

## Site Structure & Key Sections

### 1. Hero Section (Hero Section - Beenacria)
- **Concept**: An asymmetrical editorial layout.
- **Content**: Bold headline "Il miele che racconta la Sicilia" (The honey that tells the story of Sicily), Sicilian beekeeper imagery, and a primary CTA "Scopri il nostro miele".
- **Interaction**: Scroll indicator and subtle background motion.

### 2. Product Showcase (Product Showcase - Beenacria)
- **Concept**: Luxury product display.
- **Content**: High-detail product renders of the Beenacria jar with floating ingredients (chamomile, honeycomb).
- **Details**: Sensory cards for Color (Warm Amber), Flavor (Floral/Clean), Aroma (Intense), and Origin (Caltanissetta, Sicily).

### 3. The Artisanal Process (Our Process - Beenacria)
- **Concept**: A horizontal narrative journey titled "L'arte della Natura".
- **Stages**:
  1. **Fioritura (Blooming)**: Flower selection in pristine fields.
  2. **Alveare (Hive)**: The transformation of nectar into liquid gold.
  3. **Raccolta (Harvest)**: Cold extraction without forcing.
- **Style**: Glassmorphism cards with large numerical markers.

### 4. Heritage & Territory (Heritage & Territory - Beenacria)
- **Concept**: "Una piccola realtà siciliana" (A small Sicilian reality).
- **Imagery**: Fluid, organic-masked photos of the founders and the vast Sicilian landscape (Val di Noto).
- **Focus**: Connection between the bees, the sun, and the earth.

### 5. Engagement & Support (Gallery & Connect - Beenacria)
- **Gallery**: "Materia, luce, api e territorio" – a visual celebration of the apiary.
- **FAQ**: "Domande semplici, risposte sincere" – interactive accordion for storage, organic status, and shipping.
- **Contact**: Map of Sicily focusing on the Val di Noto origin, social links, and a bespoke inquiry form.

## Technical Requirements
- **Platform**: Web (Desktop focus).
- **Responsive**: Mobile-adapted versions of all editorial layouts.
- **Performance**: High-resolution image optimization for fast-loading luxury visuals.
- **Interactions**: Hover-triggered reveals, smooth scroll transitions, and micro-animations on UI elements.
